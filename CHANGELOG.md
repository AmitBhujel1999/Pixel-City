# Pixel's City — what changed in the scenarios update (1 October 2026)

## Bigger map
- The buildable map grew from 96 × 96 tiles to 160 × 160 tiles (about 2.8 times more land).
- You can zoom out further (up to 135 instead of 85) to see the whole map.
- Old saved cities still open. They are placed in the middle of the bigger map, and a road is added to link them to the highway.

## Six scenarios on the New game screen
Each scenario has its own land, water, climate, landmarks, animals, weather and building colours.

| Scenario | Start money | Climate | Landmarks and life | Special rules |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Classic valley | $30,000 | Four seasons, snowy winters | Hilltop castle, three windmills, hot air balloons, sheep, cows and deer | The original game |
| Snowy mountain town | $20,000 | −14 °C in January, 9 °C in July. Snow, blizzards | Ski gondola with moving cabins and lodges, frozen river, wolf packs, bears, deer, avalanches on the north slopes | Heating: buildings use 25% more power |
| Tropical island | $25,000 | 26–28 °C all year. Rain and tropical storms | No road reaches the island. A ferry sails in and out of a pier on the west shore. Smoking volcano (glows at night), lighthouse on a rocky islet, dolphins, seagulls, cargo ship, far islands | Each fun attraction earns $450 extra per month from tourists |
| Desert oasis | $20,000 | 14 °C in January, 37 °C in July, big day/night swing. Sandstorms | Three pyramids, Bedouin tents, a large oasis lake ringed by palm trees, camel caravans, dust devils | Water towers pump 40% less. Pumping stations only work by the lake |
| Ocean coast | $30,000 | Mild sea climate, sea fog and rain | Lighthouse on a rocky headland, cargo ship, fishing boats, dolphins, seagulls, sandy beach | Build roads over the sea as bridges |

## New weather
- **Blizzard** (snowy town): thick snow blown sideways by the wind, whiteout haze, louder wind. Avalanches happen more often during a blizzard.
- **Sandstorm** (desert): orange sky and fog, blowing sand across the screen, dust haze, louder wind.
- **Avalanche** (snowy town): every few minutes a cloud of snow slides down a mountain north of town with a rumble. A message tells you it stopped before reaching town.
- **Dust devils** (desert): spinning columns of sand wander across the dunes, stronger in a sandstorm.
- Each scenario has its own chance of each kind of weather through the year.
- In Settings > Weather you can force Blizzard in the snowy town and Sandstorm in the desert.

## Buildings match the scenario
Homes, shops and factories are repainted to fit each place:
- Snowy town: wooden walls and dark roofs.
- Tropical island: pastel walls (pink, mint, peach, sky blue) and thatch-brown roofs.
- Desert: sandstone and adobe walls and roofs, sandy gardens.
- Ocean coast: white walls with blue and terracotta roofs.
- Rich sandbox: cream and marble walls with gold roofs.

Trees also change: pines in the snow, palm trees on the island and coast, cactus in the desert (palms by the oasis).

## Tutorial for new players
- The New game screen has a "Show me how to play" box (on by default).
- A card at the top of the screen guides you through 10 steps: build a road, zone homes, add power, add water, create jobs, grow to 40 people, add a park and a school, money and taxes.
- Each step finishes on its own when you do it. The tool you need glows.
- Skip it at any time. Start it again from Settings > Tutorial.

## Fixes
- The sea on the island and coast now joins the open sea with no visible seam or green line.
- Build grid lines no longer show through the water.
- Sand strips no longer appear along the edge of the map.
- Opening a city from a different scenario reloads the page once to build the right landscape, then opens the city directly.
