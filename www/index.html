    Pixel City

    Every city starts as an empty valley with one highway coming in from the west. Link a road to it, zone homes, shops and industry beside your roads, then add power. New residents drive in along the highway. Water lets lots grow taller; parks, schools, clinics, police and a bigger population unlock towers.

    Power, water and garbage travel under your roads, so plants, pumps and landfills can sit anywhere your roads reach, even far out of town. The meters on the bar show how much the city uses against what you make: yellow for power (MW), blue for water (litres a day), brown for garbage (tonnes a day and how full the landfill is). Tap a meter for details. Buildings furthest from a plant lose supply first when you run short.

    Pick Power, Water or Garbage and the map shows who is supplied. Health, fire, police and schools still cover the area around them. The layers button has more views, like pollution and crime. Click a building to see its card right above it.

    Building and growing earns XP. Levels unlock bigger buildings and roads (medium at level 10, large at level 25) and every level-up pays prize money.

    Everyone has a daily routine. Kids walk to school in the morning, adults commute to offices and factories, and people head to shops and parks at lunch and home in the evening. With no tool picked, click anyone to follow their trip.

    Fires, sickness, crime and trash appear as the city grows, and fire engines, ambulances, police and garbage trucks drive out to deal with them. The speech-bubble button shows what people are posting about your city.

    On a person or vehicle card, Follow keeps them in view from behind, and Chase view rides along right behind them. Your cities save on this device, and My cities in settings switches between them.
